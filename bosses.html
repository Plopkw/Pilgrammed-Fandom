Bosses

Bosses are high health-point enemies that spawn throughout the world of Pilgrammed. They often spawn within their respective biome, and possess a health bar that appears on the player's HUD at the top of the screen. Killing a boss rewards the player with a unique kill screen, as well as an achievement* for the respective boss. Bosses often drop crafting materials and weapons that cannot be obtained through merchants or by other mobs.

*Not all bosses have achievements as of update rain, but they will still have a unique kill screen.

In multiplayer, all bosses will increase their max health the more players there are, with the formula of:

$$M \times \left(\frac{321}{250}\right)^n$$

where $M$ = original max health and $n$ = number of players - 1. This formula applies to all bosses except for Democan, who simply gains 5000 health for every player in the Pitfall.

There are 21 "main" bosses (bosses with a public achievement) and 16 "secret" bosses (bosses that do not have an achievement). The bosses in this list are ordered in how it's order in the bestiary.
